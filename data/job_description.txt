Position: Junior Data Scientist

We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist to join our data science team.

Requirements:

- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Computer Science,
  Information Technology or related field.
- 1+ years of experience is preferred.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- SQL knowledge.
- Experience with Pandas and NumPy.
- Knowledge of Machine Learning.
- Experience with Scikit-learn, TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Good communication skills.

Responsibilities:

- Analyze business datasets.
- Develop machine learning models.
- Clean and preprocess data.
- Prepare reports and visualizations.
- Work with other teams to solve business problems.
